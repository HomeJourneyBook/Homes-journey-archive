# Reaper

<img src="Reaper.gif" style="float: left; margin-right: 16px; margin-bottom: 8px; width: 400px;" alt="Reaper">
There are Worlds one does not return from. Not because survival is impossible — but because Jeet changes those who linger too long. Most Travelers know this, and I make a point of never sending anyone there.

And yet, Reaper goes exactly there.

His first reports shocked me. Brief, without unnecessary words — and yet he described Worlds he had traveled through at length, though they were already distorted by Jeet and on the verge of collapse. He described them as calmly as others might describe morning fog over a river. I do not know what drew him to search in such places, but he always asks me to send him to the most anomalous zones.

His conviction, as far as I can tell, is simple: civilizations hide what matters most before the end. Not in ruins, not in artifacts — but in precisely the places no one dares enter afterward.
