# The Artchemist

<img src="Artchemist.png" class="img-right" alt="Artchemist">
Artchemist is one of the Villagers responsible for preserving visual records within the Valley. He also helps me make Chaetron. And always to help in creating this Archive.

Most sketches and pictures, symbols, and fragments of distant Worlds are believed to have passed through his workshop at some point.

Unlike ordinary artists, Artchemist is primarily concerned with preserving impressions, forms, and memories through physical materials.
