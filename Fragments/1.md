# Ink

By the way, throughout my notes I use ink of the color #C8A84B.

Ink of the color #0C1113 is generally used for page background and for those occasions when Essence must be represented. A different shade of ink is reserved for Jeet — #08050A.

If anyone is curious about the origin of these inks, they are brought to us by Tealer from distant regions, exchanged for tea and other goods required by the Village.

Unfortunately, every attempt to learn more from him about the lands beyond the Valley and other known forms of life always ends the same way. Whenever questioned, he claims such information is a “trade secret.”

Damn merchant...
