# Travelers Visiting the Valley

For the record, I do not intentionally create or send Schemes of the Path to the Tavern that would lead Travelers to the Valley.

We are always glad to welcome visitors here, although such occasions are so rare that I can scarcely recall any of them. In fact, the only one that comes to mind is Asleks, one of the Unique Travelers.

Even his visit was enough to disrupt our usual rhythm for a time, so perhaps it is best that things continue at their natural pace.

Besides, why would Travelers need to come here in the first place? The Valley is perfectly capable of being studied without their assistance.
